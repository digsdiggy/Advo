About AdvoConnect

Advocacy starts
with listening.

We work alongside people, helping them understand information, explore their options, express their views and communicate what matters to them.

AdvoConnect is a Scottish Charitable Incorporated Organisation (SCIO) established to advance health and relieve those in need by reason of ill-health through the provision of independent mental health advocacy in Scotland.

We support people affected by mental disorder to have their views, wishes and concerns heard, particularly when decisions are being made about their mental health, care and treatment.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide independent mental health advocacy for people affected by mental disorder in Scotland, helping people understand their rights, express their views and participate meaningfully in decisions about their care and treatment.

We work alongside people to help ensure that their voices are heard and that they are supported to participate in decisions that affect their lives.

Looking ahead

A society where
everyone has a voice.

We envision a Scotland where people affected by mental disorder are listened to, treated with dignity and respect, and able to participate meaningfully in decisions about their care and treatment.

We want people to have access to independent mental health advocacy when they need support to understand information, express their views or have their voice heard.

What guides us

Our Objectives

Our objectives are centred on improving people's ability to participate, make informed choices and have their voices heard.

Provide independent mental health advocacy to people affected by mental disorder in Scotland.

Support people to express their views, wishes and concerns about their mental health, care and treatment.

Help people understand information relating to decisions about their mental health, care and treatment.

Support people to participate meaningfully in decisions that affect their lives.

Promote people's rights, dignity, equality, respect and inclusion.

Help reduce barriers that may prevent people affected by mental disorder from having their voices heard.

Provide advocacy particularly for people who are subject to, or affected by, the Mental Health (Care and Treatment) (Scotland) Act 2003.

Work collaboratively with individuals, communities and relevant organisations in support of independent advocacy.

Learn from people's experiences to improve the quality and accessibility of independent mental health advocacy.

Your rights

Mental Health Act
& advocacy.

If you are affected by mental disorder in Scotland, you may have a right to access independent advocacy. AdvoConnect supports people to understand information, express their views and participate in decisions about their care and treatment.

Mental Health (Care and Treatment) (Scotland) Act 2003

Your voice should be part of decisions about your care.

Section 259 of the Mental Health (Care and Treatment) (Scotland) Act 2003 provides people with a mental disorder with a right of access to independent advocacy.

Independent advocacy can help you communicate your views, wishes and concerns and have as much involvement as possible in decisions about your care and welfare.
